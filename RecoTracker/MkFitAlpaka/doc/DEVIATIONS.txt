MkFitAlpaka: physics differences to the reference mkFit, for the mkFit developers
=================================================================================
Reference: mkFit (MkFitCore, MkFitCMS, MkFit) with the final fit of trackingMkFitFit as MkFitCore has it (its own
propagation and update sequences, the field constants fitted to the field map, per-module material, the outlier rules
and the per-hit / outer states), MkFitCore built with -Ofast, and the release JSON of the LST step
(mkfit-phase2-lstStep.json). Everything not listed here reproduces the reference at the rounding level: every stage
was compared with the reference on the same events against its own rounding differences (MkFitCore built for
x86-64-v3 vs v2 and with / without -Ofast).
Each entry: what the reference does, why it is wrong or worse, what the port does, the evidence, the switch.
Code markers: "// DEVIATION DEV-<n>". ON = applied by customizeHLTforMkFitAlpaka.

Earlier entries now in the reference (the port follows MkFitCore there; numbers kept for the cross-references):
  DEV-1  NaN guards of the final fit: MkFitCore tests isFinite, which -Ofast does not fold away.
  DEV-3  outlier test of the innermost / outermost hit with the pass that predicts it (chi2 > 20), and outlier rounds
         until a round removes no hit.
  DEV-4  a fit with a non-finite or negative chi2 has failed (chi2 -1 after a failed backward pass, or an outlier kept
         by the three-hit minimum): the output conversion drops it.
  DEV-5  every fit pass propagates to its first hit (not only the refits after an outlier removal).
  DEV-6  the track algorithm of the output conversion is a parameter (algorithmName, 'initialStep' in the menu).

DEV-2  Seed states of the LST T5 / T4 / pT3 / pT5 seeds from a device Kalman fit (ON; known small regression)
  Reference: LSTOutputConverter.cc:236-262 runs SeedFromConsecutiveHitsCreator::makeSeed on the host (FastHelix + initial
    errors + one forward KF with PropagatorWithMaterial and the track-angle hit refit), MkFitSeedConverter.cc:107-160
    converts the state. The largest host cost of the step.
  Port: the host creator's structure on the device with mkFit's propagation and material (src/alpaka/seeds/
    LstSeedKernels.h: FastHelix through the region origin, its initial errors, one forward pass, state at the last
    hit, its rejections). A rejected T5 / T4 seed is dropped, a rejected pT3 / pT5 seed keeps its pixel seed (hits and
    the DEV-7 state), as the converter (LSTOutputConverter.cc:268-272, :279). pLS seeds: DEV-7.
  Evidence: paired efficiency -0.00013 (ttbar) / -0.00014 (QCD) +- 0.00001; every MTV bin within 2 sigma; fake and
    duplicate rates equal or lower.

DEV-7  pLS parameters from a device emulation of the pixel-seed creator, computed before LST (ON; known regression)
  Reference: hltInitialStepSeeds (SeedGeneratorFromProtoTracksEDProducer + SeedFromConsecutiveHitsCreator on the pixel
    tracks) -> hltInputLST (state on the last hit, TSCBL, perigee ptError / etaError) -> LST's pLS. ~19 ms host.
  Port: hltInputLSTDevice does the same per pixel track on the device (DEV-2's creator emulation, the device PCA, the
    same error formulas); a device creator failure gives no pLS, as the host creator gives no seed.
  Evidence: pLS fields vs the host: r3PCA q90 0.18 mm, dz q90 0.67 mm, ptPCA q90 2.6%; endcap ptErr wider (|eta| > 2.5:
    q50 +10%). Paired efficiency ttbar -0.00030 +- 0.00030, QCD -0.00035 +- 0.00026 (hltGeneral); MTV within 2 sigma;
    pulls and resolutions 3 of 960 tests beyond 3 sigma. With the KF-menu comparison: -0.05 (ttbar) / -0.02 (QCD) pp
    at 0.9-10 GeV, from |eta| > 2.5. The remaining difference enters through LST's pLS selection
    (ptIn > ptCut - 2 ptErr) and the endcap ptErr; refitting the pixel hits with the track angles matches the host
    fields but loses forward efficiency (tried, not used).

DEV-8  Seed hit order: equal sort keys in input order (ON)
  Reference: LSTOutputConverter std::sorts the seed hits (inner tracker first, subdetector, det radius / |z|). Hits with
    equal keys (e.g. overlapping modules of one layer or disk ring) keep their input order in lists of up to 16 hits;
    in longer lists their order is libstdc++'s introsort order.
  Port: equal keys in input order for any list length: a rank within each LST logical layer in the device seed
    hand-off.
  Evidence: bitwise equal to a host hand-off with std::stable_sort in 294,694 ttbar + 433,155 QCD seeds (CPU, 200 events
    each). Vs LSTOutputConverter's order 1.4% (ttbar) / 1.5% (QCD) of the seeds differ, all with > 16 hits. Physics vs
    the std::sort order (GPU, 500 ttbar + 500 QCD, paired): efficiency +0.01 +- 0.01 / +0.02 +- 0.02, displaced
    2.5-63 cm +0.00 +- 0.03 / +0.06 +- 0.03, fake and duplicate rates unchanged.

DEV-10 Final-fit outlier test near the outer end, one outlier per round (ON, nearEdgeHitsOuter 3, outliersPerRound 1)
  MkFitCore: MkBuilder::fit_tracks (outlier block): the outermost hit is an outlier when the forward pass has chi2 > 20,
    the other hits only when both one-sided chi2 are large. Near the outer end the backward side is predicted from 1-2
    hits plus the x100 prior, so hits 1-2 from the end are never removed, and all flagged hits of a round go at once.
    KFFittingSmoother (EstimateCut 20, MaxNumberOfOutliers 6) removes one outlier per iteration, near-end hits
    included.
  Port: the nearEdgeHitsOuter (3) hits nearest the outer end are outliers when the forward pass has chi2 > 20; at most
    outliersPerRound (1) hit per round, the largest f + b first; rounds continue until a round removes no hit, as in
    MkFitCore. Two conditions keep the test to forward predictions that can test a hit:
    - an outer hit, the outermost one included, is tested only when the hits before it lie on at least 3 other
      detector layers (the two sensors of a stacked module are one layer; three points fix the circle);
    - a hit only this window flags counts only in a round in which no other hit of the track is flagged. On short
      displaced tracks the forward prediction at the outer hits is built on hits that still hold an outlier; without
      this condition true outer hits were removed (production radius 31.6-63 cm: -0.8 pp ttbar, -1.0 pp QCD).
    GPU: the first 6 rounds run as stage kernels, the tracks still refitting then finish in one tail kernel with the
    same calls per track.
  Evidence: 2000 ttbar + 1000 QCD events with the fine-grained material, GPU, paired per simulated track against the
    rule OFF: hltGeneral efficiency (pT > 1 GeV) +0.16 +- 0.01 pp (ttbar) / +0.18 +- 0.02 pp (QCD); production radius
    31.6-63 cm +0.35 +- 0.17 / -0.07 +- 0.13 pp; fake rate 0.98 -> 0.92% / 1.22 -> 1.17%; pT pull core width unchanged.

DEV-11 Track building with the field constants fitted to the field map (ON, MkFitAlpakaESProducer buildingFitField)
  MkFitCore: track building bends with Bz from the constants of CMSSW's ParabolicMf (MkFitGeometryESProducer copies
    portableParabolicMagneticField::Parameters into Config::mag_*); the final fit uses its own constants, fitted to the
    CMS field map over the tracker volume (Config::mag_fit_*). The ParabolicMf constants are low by 1.46% on average
    against that map (up to 4.7% at the ends of the tracker), so the building's pT estimate is low by about as much.
  Port: track building uses the final fit's constants (MaterialView::bField = mag_fit_*); the final fit and the device
    seed fits are unchanged.
  Evidence: 500 ttbar + 500 QCD PU200 events, L40, paired over files, the same code with and without the switch:
    hltGeneral efficiency at sim pT 0.8-1 GeV +0.69 +- 0.11 (ttbar) / +0.73 +- 0.12 (QCD) absolute %, above 1 GeV
    unchanged (+0.01 +- 0.01 / -0.01 +- 0.02), fake rate -0.005 +- 0.001 / +0.002 +- 0.002, +14.5 / +14.2 initial-step
    tracks per event. The low-pT tracks pass the building's candidate pT cut (0.85 / 0.8 GeV) with the unbiased pT.
