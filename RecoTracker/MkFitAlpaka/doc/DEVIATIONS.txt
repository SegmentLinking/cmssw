MkFitAlpaka: physics differences to the reference mkFit, for the mkFit developers
=================================================================================
Reference: CMSSW_20_1_0_pre2 mkFit (MkFitCore, MkFitCMS, MkFit) with the runtime field and per-module material of the final fit,
MkFitCore built with -Ofast, the fine-grained Phase-2 material map (Brunella D'Anzi's PR) and the release JSON of the
LST step (mkfit-phase2-lstStep.json). Everything not listed here reproduces the reference at the rounding level: every
stage was compared with the reference on the same events against its own rounding differences (MkFitCore built for
x86-64-v3 vs v2 and with / without -Ofast).
Each entry: what the reference does (file:line), why it is wrong or worse, what the port does, the evidence, the switch.
Code markers: "// DEVIATION DEV-<n>". ON = applied by customizeHLTforMkFitAlpaka.

DEV-1  NaN guards of the final fit are dead in the -Ofast MkFitCore build (ON, no switch; port = MkFitCore source)
  MkFitCore: MkFitter.cc:102 reFitOutputTracks "if (m_Chi2 != m_Chi2) continue;" and MkBuilder.cc:1545-1548 (fit_tracks,
    NaN chi2 -> 1e6) are folded away under -ffinite-math-only (-Ofast): NaN states are written into the track, NaN
    per-hit chi2 enter the outlier ranking (std::map keys).
  Port: the same checks (src/alpaka/fit/FitKernels.h); the device code is built without -ffinite-math-only.
  Evidence: replay ttbar 100 events, 193,069 fitted tracks: 59 MkFitCore tracks with NaN parameters, 0 in the port.
  Proposed MkFitCore fix: a bit-test isNaN that survives -Ofast, used at both places (then MkFitCore == port).

DEV-2  Seed states of the LST T5 / T4 / pT3 / pT5 seeds from a device Kalman fit (ON; known small regression)
  Reference: LSTOutputConverter.cc:236-262 runs SeedFromConsecutiveHitsCreator::makeSeed on the host (FastHelix + initial
    errors + one forward KF with PropagatorWithMaterial and the track-angle hit refit), MkFitSeedConverter.cc:107-160
    converts the state. The largest host cost of the step.
  Port: the host creator's structure on the device with mkFit's propagation and material (src/alpaka/seeds/
    LstSeedKernels.h: FastHelix through the region origin, its initial errors, one forward pass, state at the last
    hit, its rejections). A rejected T5 / T4 seed is dropped, a rejected pT3 / pT5 seed keeps its pixel seed (hits and
    the DEV-7 state), as the converter (LSTOutputConverter.cc:268-272, :279). pLS seeds: DEV-7.
  Evidence: paired efficiency -0.00013 (ttbar) / -0.00014 (QCD) +- 0.00001; every MTV bin within 2 sigma; fake and
    duplicate rates equal or lower.

DEV-3  Final-fit outlier test also at the first / last hit, and iterated removal (ON, edgeOutliers + outlierRounds 3)
  MkFitCore: MkBuilder.cc fit_tracks (outlier block :1540-1575, remap :1660-1680): a hit is an outlier only when both
    one-sided chi2 are large (pT > 1: f > 8 && b > 8 && f + b > 20). At the first and last fitted hit one pass starts
    from the x100 prior, so its chi2 is ~0 and an end hit is never removed; one round, the refit is never checked.
    KFFittingSmoother rejects any hit with chi2 > 20, end hits included, and iterates.
  Port: the end hit is an outlier when the chi2 predicted from all other hits is > 20 (edgeChi2Cut); removal and refit
    up to 3 rounds (FitKernels.h removeOutliers / outliersAndRefit).
  Evidence: with DEV-4 + DEV-5 and the fine-grained material, hltGeneral efficiency (absolute %): +0.27 (ttbar) /
    +0.26 (QCD) vs the reference mkFit fit; the mkFit-fit menu stays -0.21 +- 0.02 (ttbar) / -0.19 +- 0.04 (QCD) below the
    KF menu. 500 ttbar + 500 QCD events: fake rate down, no MTV bin worse by > 2 sigma.
  Note: this rule makes the MkFitCore fit itself ~7x more sensitive to rounding (MkFitCore + this rule vs itself across build
    flags: 5.5e-4 of the hit lists differ instead of 7.6e-5); DEV-5 removes most of it.

DEV-4  Candidates with a negative or non-finite fit chi2 are dropped in the output conversion (ON, dropNegativeChi2)
  Reference: MkFitOutputTrackConverter converts any candidate that passes the state checks, whatever its chi2; such chi2
    come from a non-positive-definite covariance, which KFFittingSmoother rejects.
  Port: MkFitAlpakaOutputTrackConverter skips them after the positive-definite check.
  Evidence: 2 tracks in 500 ttbar events; its share of the DEV-3 gain is ~0.

DEV-5  A refit after outlier removal propagates to its first hit (ON, firstHitProp 1)
  MkFitCore (MkFitter.cc:268 fwdFitFitTracks, :462 bkReFitFitTracks): the first hit of a pass is updated without
    propagation ("already at the hit"). After outlier removal of the innermost hit that is false: the first update is
    made off the hit's plane (5.0% of the refits in MkFitCore, median 0.68 cm; first-step chi2 median 2.2e3) and 4.4% of
    those end non-positive-definite.
  Port: the refits after outlier removal propagate to their first hit (FitKernels.h fitDirection, propFirst).
  Evidence: MkFitCore with the same flag: first-step chi2 median 0.93, negative / NaN chi2 sums 71 -> 6 (ttbar 200);
    MkFitCore MTV +0.00024 (ttbar), 0 bins beyond 2 sigma. In the port with DEV-3: CUDA-vs-serial hit-list differences
    98 -> 16 per 193k tracks, paired efficiency +0.00005 over DEV-3 + DEV-4.

DEV-6  Explicit track algorithm 'initialStep' in the output conversion (ON, algorithm)
  Reference: MkFitOutputTrackConverter.cc:213-214 (and MkFitOutputConverter.cc:183) derive the algorithm from the seeds
    label; hltInitialStepTrajectorySeedsLST gives undefAlgorithm. PFTrackAlgoTools::errorScale gives undefAlgorithm a
    1e9 pT-error scale, so PFAlgo's rejectTracks_Bad drops ~12 good tracks per ttbar event.
  Port: the algorithm is a parameter, set to the KF menu's 'initialStep'.
  Evidence: tracks identical; tracks not used by PF 11.9 -> 0.3 per event (KF 0.6); PF charged sum pT, HT, MET and the
    HT / MET / b-tag / tau paths move to the KF values.

DEV-7  pLS parameters from a device emulation of the pixel-seed creator, computed before LST (ON; known regression)
  Reference: hltInitialStepSeeds (SeedGeneratorFromProtoTracksEDProducer + SeedFromConsecutiveHitsCreator on the pixel
    tracks) -> hltInputLST (state on the last hit, TSCBL, perigee ptError / etaError) -> LST's pLS. ~19 ms host.
  Port: hltInputLSTDevice does the same per pixel track on the device (DEV-2's creator emulation, the device PCA, the
    same error formulas); a device creator failure gives no pLS, as the host creator gives no seed.
  Evidence: pLS fields vs the host: r3PCA q90 0.18 mm, dz q90 0.67 mm, ptPCA q90 2.6%; endcap ptErr wider (|eta| > 2.5:
    q50 +10%). Paired efficiency ttbar -0.00030 +- 0.00030, QCD -0.00035 +- 0.00026 (hltGeneral); MTV within 2 sigma;
    pulls and resolutions 3 of 960 tests beyond 3 sigma. With the KF-menu comparison: -0.05 (ttbar) / -0.02 (QCD) pp
    at 0.9-10 GeV, from |eta| > 2.5. The remaining difference enters through LST's pLS selection
    (ptIn > ptCut - 2 ptErr) and the endcap ptErr; refitting the pixel hits with the track angles matches the host
    fields but loses forward efficiency (tried, not used).

DEV-8  Seed hit order: equal sort keys in input order (ON)
  Reference: LSTOutputConverter std::sorts the seed hits (inner tracker first, subdetector, det radius / |z|). Hits with
    equal keys (e.g. overlapping modules of one layer or disk ring) keep their input order in lists of up to 16 hits;
    in longer lists their order is libstdc++'s introsort order.
  Port: equal keys in input order for any list length: a rank within each LST logical layer in the device seed
    hand-off.
  Evidence: bitwise equal to a host hand-off with std::stable_sort in 294,694 ttbar + 433,155 QCD seeds (CPU, 200 events
    each). Vs LSTOutputConverter's order 1.4% (ttbar) / 1.5% (QCD) of the seeds differ, all with > 16 hits. Physics vs
    the std::sort order (GPU, 500 ttbar + 500 QCD, paired): efficiency +0.01 +- 0.01 / +0.02 +- 0.02, displaced
    2.5-63 cm +0.00 +- 0.03 / +0.06 +- 0.03, fake and duplicate rates unchanged.

DEV-10 Final-fit outlier test near the outer end, one outlier per round (ON, NEAR_END_OUTLIERS)
  MkFitCore: MkBuilder.cc fit_tracks (outlier block): a hit is an outlier only when both one-sided chi2 are large. Near a
    track end one side is predicted from 1-2 hits plus the x100 prior, so hits 1-3 from an end are never removed (DEV-3
    tests the end hit only), and all flagged hits of a round go at once. KFFittingSmoother (EstimateCut 20,
    MaxNumberOfOutliers 6) removes one outlier per iteration, near-end hits included.
  Port: with DEV-3, the nearEdgeHitsOuter (3) hits nearest the outer end are outliers when the forward pass has
    chi2 > edgeChi2Cut; at most outliersPerRound (1) hit per round, the largest f + b first; rounds continue until a
    round removes no hit (outlierRounds 0), as the KF fit-smoother's outlier loop, which has no iteration cap
    (KFFittingSmootherESProducer.cc fitOne). Every round removes a hit and at least 3 stay, so a track takes at most
    nFoundHits - 3 rounds (FitKernels.h removeOutliers / outliersAndRefit). Two conditions keep the test to forward
    predictions that can test a hit:
    - an outer hit, the end hit included, is tested only when the hits before it lie on at least 3 other detector
      layers (the two sensors of a stacked module are one layer; three points fix the circle);
    - a hit only this window flags counts only in a round in which no other hit of the track is flagged. On short
      displaced tracks the forward prediction at the outer hits is built on hits that still hold an outlier; without
      this condition true outer hits were removed (production radius 31.6-63 cm: -0.8 pp ttbar, -1.0 pp QCD).
    nearEdgeHitsInner stays 1: at the inner end the backward prediction of hits 1-2 lacks the innermost hits that fix
    dxy / dz and flags good pixel hits (with 3: dxy / dz q68 +1-3%, 1.1% of the tracks lose a pixel hit).
    With a cap of 6 rounds, candidates ending in a run of 7-11 wrong outer hits kept part of the run and lost true
    inner hits instead. Without it, 0.18% (ttbar) / 0.19% (QCD) of the tracks take more than 6 rounds, at most 16 / 18.
    GPU: the first 6 rounds run as stage kernels, the tracks still refitting then finish in one tail kernel with the
    same calls per track. Without the cap, paired per simulated track on 332 ttbar + 500 QCD events (CPU): hltGeneral
    efficiency (pT > 1 GeV) +0.044 +- 0.014 pp (ttbar) / +0.039 +- 0.010 pp (QCD), pT > 10 GeV +0.14 / +0.07 pp,
    production radius 2.5-31.6 cm +0.11 / +0.05 pp, no region lower; GPU (ttbar) +0.031 +- 0.014 pp.
  Evidence: 2000 ttbar + 1000 QCD events with the fine-grained material, GPU, paired per simulated track against the
    rule OFF: hltGeneral efficiency (pT > 1 GeV) +0.16 +- 0.01 pp (ttbar) / +0.18 +- 0.02 pp (QCD); production radius
    31.6-63 cm +0.35 +- 0.17 / -0.07 +- 0.13 pp; fake rate 0.98 -> 0.92% / 1.22 -> 1.17%; pT pull core width unchanged
    (1.078). The remaining efficiency difference to the KF menu: -0.22 -> -0.06 pp (ttbar), -0.30 -> -0.12 pp (QCD).
